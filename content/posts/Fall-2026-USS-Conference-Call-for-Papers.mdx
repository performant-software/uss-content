Universities Studying Slavery (USS) will host its fall conference in College Park on the campus of the University of Maryland October 22-25. 

The 2026 conference theme is “250 Years of Freedom and Unfreedom: Truth, Liberty, and Justice for All.”

They invite proposals that engage with:

● How people worked to achieve personal and national notions of freedom.

● How the struggle for freedom changed across time, place, and racial/ethnic groups.

● How education and freedom are interconnected histories.

● The responsibilities of archivists and librarians in truth telling.

● The ways freedom is linked to healing practices.

● The ways historical figures engaged with liberty in both life and death.

● Current and past preservation methods that influenced our historical memory.

● The progress Universities Studying Slavery makes in engaging with truth, liberty, and justice.

Proposal Guidelines: Individual papers should include a 250-word abstract and a 100-word maximum biography. Poster proposals should include a 100-word abstract and biography. Organized sessions, individual papers, and individual posters are welcome. If you are part of an organized session, please include the proposed session title, the organizer's name and the organizer's email contact in the appropriate location on the proposal submission form. Proposals are welcome from academic and public history professionals, librarians and archivists, undergraduate and graduate students, and all others with work related to the conference themes. Advanced registration and associated fees are required for presenters following notification of acceptance.

Please share this CFP with colleagues/interested persons and follow this [link](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfUe47k0J5JusUi2scQ5jFkkKYxUK9Dt77D02Xz9BCMpHffmA/closedform) to submit a proposal. The deadline for submissions is Friday, January 30, 2026. Presenters will be notified by April 10, 2026. For questions, please email [1856umdproject@umd.edu](mailto:1856umdproject@umd.edu).
